The Process
Simple. Fast. Reliable.

From inquiry to delivery — our streamlined process ensures you receive the right device without delays or confusion.

01

Submit Your Request

Fill out our rental form with the prescribed flow rate, duration, and your delivery address. A prescription or doctor's note may be needed for clinical-grade devices.

02

Device Selection & Confirmation

Our clinical team reviews your requirements and recommends the most suitable device. You receive a confirmation call within 30 minutes of your request.

03

Sanitisation & Dispatch

Every device is thoroughly sanitised, calibrated, and quality-checked before leaving our facility. A trained technician accompanies delivery to install and brief the patient.

04

Ongoing Support & Pickup

24/7 clinical helpline available throughout your rental. At the end of your rental period, we collect the device — no hassle, no hidden fees.
